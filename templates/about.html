OUR STORY & VALUES

About Jahangeer Chicken Center

Serving fresh, high-quality chicken products to individual households, restaurants, caterers, and businesses across Secunderabad.

Your Trusted Local Chicken Center

Jahangeer Chicken Center is a locally-owned fresh chicken shop located opposite the Army College of Dental Sciences at Rajiv Swagruha ABHIMAAN Project, Secunderabad.

We specialize in providing farm-fresh chicken cuts tailored to your exact culinary needs. From whole chicken and custom curry cuts to pure boneless chicken and organ cuts (liver, gizzard), we ensure clean, hygienic preparation for every single order.

Our Chicken Supplier Relationship

Jahangeer Chicken Center purchases top-quality fresh chicken supplied by Sneha Fresh Chicken, one of South India's premier poultry providers known for strict biosecurity and quality standards.

Note: Jahangeer Chicken Center is an independent retail & wholesale shop purchasing fresh chicken supplied by Sneha.
